Anoti · Privacy Policy

Anoti Support

Anoti lets you know when new episodes of the anime you follow come out. Browse what's airing now and what's coming soon, or search the catalog, and tap the bell to follow a title. Anoti then checks for new episodes in the background and sends you a notification. The Favorites screen keeps the titles you follow in one place, with a counter for the episodes you've watched.

Anoti is free on Android and iOS, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account. It is made by Aleksei Vinogradov, an independent developer.

Contact

Common questions

Notifications don't arrive

Check these in order:

  1. Notifications are allowed. On iPhone, open Settings > Notifications > Anoti and turn on Allow Notifications. On Android, touch and hold the Anoti icon, tap App info, then Notifications. Make sure notifications are on, including the "Anime notification channel" category. If notifications are off, Anoti may remind you with a "Very nyanportant alert" when it starts. Tap "Kawaii nya" to allow them or to open the settings. On Android 13 and later, Android stops asking after you refuse twice, so turn notifications on in the settings.
  2. You follow at least one title. Only titles with the bell turned on are checked.
  3. Your phone is online. The check needs an internet connection.
  4. iPhone: Background App Refresh is on. Open Settings > General > Background App Refresh. Tap Background App Refresh at the top and choose Wi-Fi or Wi-Fi & Cellular Data. Then turn on the switch next to Anoti. If you've just turned it on, close Anoti in the app switcher and open it again, so it can schedule its checks. Low Power Mode pauses these checks. So does swiping Anoti away in the app switcher, until you open it again. iOS decides when checks run. The first comes at least an hour after you open Anoti, and later ones can be further apart.
  5. Android: battery limits. Anoti checks about once an hour. Some phones limit background work to save battery. Open Anoti's App info, tap Battery (App battery usage on newer phones) and choose Unrestricted. On recent Pixels, turn on Allow background usage first. On Android 10 and 11, open Settings > Apps & notifications > Special app access > Battery optimization, show all apps, and set Anoti to Don't optimize. Phone makers may name these differently.
  6. Android: unused-app pausing is off. If you rarely open Anoti, Android may pause it after a few months, and the checks stop. In Anoti's App info, turn off "Pause app activity if unused" (Android 13 and later) or "Remove permissions and free up space" (Android 12).

To check right away, pull down on the Favorites screen. You get a notification only if an episode came out since the last check. While Anoti is open, the notification goes to the notification list without a pop-up. Pulling down also clears the new-episode markers on the Favorites screen.

Where does the information come from?

From Shikimori, a public anime database. Titles, episode counts, release dates, scores and posters appear as Shikimori publishes them. A mistake there also shows up in Anoti, and a new episode appears only after Shikimori records it.

Why can't I find a title?

The On air and Soon lists show only titles that are airing or announced. Use search to find finished series. Anoti also always hides explicit titles (rated Rx on Shikimori), and this filter can't be turned off. Titles rated R (17+) and R+ (mild nudity) are not hidden.

Do I need an account?

No. Anoti has no accounts or sign-in.

What data does Anoti collect?

None reaches me. Your followed titles and watched-episode counts are saved on your phone. To load the catalog, run your searches and check for new episodes, the app asks Shikimori directly. The Privacy Policy lists what those requests carry.

How do I delete my data?

  • One title: turn its bell off.
  • Everything on Android: open Anoti's App info, tap Storage (Storage & cache on some phones), then Clear storage. Uninstalling works too, but see the note on backups below.
  • Everything on iPhone: delete the app.

Your phone's backup may still hold a copy of your followed titles. On Android, reinstalling Anoti can bring the list back from your Google backup. On iPhone, the copy stays in iCloud or computer backups until they're replaced.
